Questions travellers ask

How do I pay?

[Pay the driver in cash at the end of the ride, in Sri Lankan rupees or US dollars.] [Card: yes / no.] [Deposit: no deposit needed / X% deposit by bank transfer.] The price is fixed when we confirm, so you know the full amount before you get in the car.

What if my flight is late?

No problem, and no extra cost. We track your flight number, so your driver knows when you really land. Your free waiting time ([60] minutes) starts when the plane lands, not at the time you booked.

Can we stop on the way?

Yes. Tell us where you want to stop when you book. [Short stops for food, toilets and photos are free.] [Longer stops or detours: $X per extra hour, added to your fixed price before you travel.]

Is the price per person or per car?

Per car. One person or three people in a car pay the same price. A van takes up to 8 people for one van price.

Do you have child seats?

Yes, on request and at no extra cost. Tell us your child's age and weight when you book, so we bring the right seat.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancellation is free up to [24] hours before pickup. [Later than that: your policy here.] To cancel, just send us a WhatsApp message.

Is it safe to travel at night?

Our drivers do airport pickups at night often, and the expressways are fine at night. On mountain roads, for example to Ella, we prefer daylight because of fog, sharp bends and animals on the road. If your flight lands late, we can take you to a hotel near the airport and continue in the morning.
