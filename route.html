On the way

Good stops on the way

Without stops, we take the expressway. With stops, we take the coast road (Galle Road). It is slower, but it runs next to the sea. Tell us your stops when you book and we include them in the fixed price.

  1. +[1h]

    Bentota

    A beach and river town about halfway. A good place for lunch and a swim.

  2. +[45m]

    Madu River, Balapitiya

    Boat rides through the mangroves and small islands. Boat tickets are paid at the river.

  3. +[30m]

    Kosgoda

    Sea turtle hatcheries next to the beach. Entrance is paid at the gate.

Questions about Colombo Airport to Galle

How long is the drive from Colombo Airport to Galle?

Yes. The price includes all tolls, fuel and the driver. You pay nothing extra on the road.
